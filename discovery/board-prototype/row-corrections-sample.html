No dimming, and the slot header both ways

Nothing on a substitute row is dimmed — it has a plate appearance, a row identity and the same equations as every other row, and it is the row the footnote points at. The indent and the rule carry the signal. The position column's header is blank, as a printed boxscore has it; the slot header is shown with # and blank, since no boxscore has a slot column and there is no convention to follow.
