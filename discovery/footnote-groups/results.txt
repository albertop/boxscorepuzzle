=== THE CONTROL ===
⚠️ Exactly two terms on both sides. It used to be `>= 2`, which compares populations that
   are composed differently: a multi-row group has more chances to contain events, so its
   term distribution is heavier, and a lower rate could come from carrying more terms, from
   assignment ambiguity, or from both. The aggregate could not tell them apart while the
   text beside it claimed it could — which is the confound this control exists to avoid.
  2 terms exactly:
    one row  (not ambiguous at all): 100% unique [14]
    two rows (assignment unknown):   0% unique [1]
  Term count is held fixed and only the number of possible carriers changes, so a difference
  is the assignment. The stratified table above is authoritative; this is one slice of it.
