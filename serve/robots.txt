# Two paths are disallowed here as well as carrying noindex in their own markup, and the duplication is
# deliberate: a crawler that never fetches a page never reads its meta robots, so a puzzle URL arriving
# from a shared link would be crawled before anything told it not to be.
#
# Section 7 keeps the teams and the date out of the URL and section 9 sells the archive. A crawlable
# /p/<id> is a permanent public address for a puzzle meant to be one day old and then sold.

User-agent: *
Disallow: /p/
Disallow: /today
Disallow: /ui/
Disallow: /engine/
Disallow: /data/
